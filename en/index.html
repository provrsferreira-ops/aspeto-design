FAQ

I’m already on Booking and Airbnb. Do I need a website?

Platforms bring visibility, but they take a commission on every booking. Your own site lets guests book direct and helps you keep the ones who already stayed.

Will it work with my booking engine?

In most cases, yes: we connect the site to the system you already use. If you don’t have one yet, we help you choose.
